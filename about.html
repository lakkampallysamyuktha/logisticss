Our Story

Founded in 2010, Stackly Logistics began with a simple yet ambitious vision: to transform the global supply chain through intelligent routing, cutting‑edge technology, and a relentless commitment to reliability.

What started as a small freight forwarding operation in New York has grown into a worldwide network spanning 120+ countries. Today, we move everything from time‑sensitive air cargo to massive ocean shipments, all while maintaining the personal touch that defines our brand.

We believe that logistics is more than moving goods — it's about moving businesses forward. Every shipment we handle is backed by our promise of transparency, speed, and care.

— The Stackly Team
